I developed Into The Light, a horror game that combines atmospheric storytelling with puzzle-solving mechanics. This project showcases my ability to create immersive experiences that engage players through both narrative and gameplay elements.

## Game Overview

Into The Light is a horror game where players must solve puzzles while navigating through a mysterious and atmospheric environment. The game emphasizes storytelling, exploration, and intellectual challenges over traditional horror tropes.

## Key Features

**Atmospheric Horror**: Created an immersive horror atmosphere through careful use of lighting, sound design, and environmental storytelling that builds tension without relying on jump scares.

**Puzzle Design**: Implemented a variety of puzzles that range from simple logic challenges to complex multi-step problems that require creative thinking and careful observation.

**Environmental Storytelling**: Designed levels that tell stories through environmental details, allowing players to piece together the narrative through exploration and discovery.

**Sound Design**: Developed an audio system that enhances the horror atmosphere through ambient sounds, environmental audio cues, and dynamic music that responds to player actions.

## Technical Implementation

Built with Unity and C#, the game features advanced lighting systems, efficient puzzle mechanics, and optimized performance for smooth gameplay even in complex scenes.

## Gameplay Mechanics

The game combines exploration, puzzle-solving, and narrative elements to create a cohesive experience. Players must carefully observe their surroundings, collect clues, and solve puzzles to progress through the story.

## Visual Design

Created a distinctive visual style that enhances the horror atmosphere while maintaining clear gameplay elements. The lighting system plays a crucial role in both atmosphere and puzzle mechanics.

## Player Experience

Into The Light offers a thoughtful horror experience that rewards careful observation and problem-solving. The game creates tension through atmosphere and story rather than relying on traditional horror mechanics.

This project demonstrates my ability to create atmospheric games that engage players through multiple gameplay elements while maintaining technical excellence.
